## Original narrative device: a working tree

Use the grammar of software development without merely imitating a terminal: source branches, tracked decisions, inspectable changes, and a deliberate *record of work*. Each chapter feels like a well-edited engineering notebook. A slim project index/trace rail acts as a navigation aid, not a gimmick.

Possible chapter names (UI should also retain conventional labels):

- `00 / INITIALIZE` — identity and current work.
- `01 / IN PRODUCTION` — selected operational systems.
- `02 / UNDER THE HOOD` — deeply explained case studies and architecture.
- `03 / RESEARCH LOG` — research with precise publication states.
- `04 / TEAM HISTORY` — career experience and AUSTRC leadership.
- `05 / OPEN CONNECTION` — contact.

Do not force these names into every breadcrumb or make visitors memorize jargon.

## Distinctive signatures

- **Booking Lifecycle:** an honest, client-safe, locally simulated state machine illustrating booking holds/approval, not a real booking portal.
- **Routing Under Pressure:** an interactive static visualization of how modes/anomalies affect a route, grounded in GoliTransit concepts; demo numbers must be labeled sample data.
- **The Engineering Diff:** case-study overlays that surface constraints, decisions and verified evidence (never manufactured before/after improvements).
- **Command Index:** optional accessible, functional navigation/command palette, not fake terminal theater.
- **Research Status Ledger:** accepted/presented, submitted, proposal and ongoing kept sharply distinct.

Ship 2–3 signature experiences exceptionally, not all five superficially.

## Creative constraints

- Prefer quiet sophistication over spectacle; engineering precision, strong typography, editorial layouts, purposeful whitespace.
- No dominant purple-white gradient style, pastel glass cards, floating tech badges, or stock developer illustrations.
- Avoid “AI slop” mechanically: build custom section compositions with content-specific relationships, not by selecting another boilerplate aesthetic.
- Scrolling should feel fluid yet native, accessible, and immediately responsive. No multi-second intro sequence.
- Light and dark chapters may contrast, but maintain a coherent shared typographic and spacing system.
