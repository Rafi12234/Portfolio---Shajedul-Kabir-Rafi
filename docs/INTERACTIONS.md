# Interaction Language V2 — Cinematic Editorial

**Retire old terminal/command-palette-as-identity, fake IDE, git-branch motif.** Luxury magazine/editorial UI is the theme; actual software-engineering evidence remains legitimate subject matter.

## Global controls
Header links, mobile navigation, CTA, project/media links, disclosures and social links get carefully timed 120–260ms refined line/underline/position states and visible keyboard focus. No inaccessible hover-only content or custom-cursor gimmicks.

## Signature motion
- Hero: cream monumental mask headline, burgundy disc reveal, authentic Rafi portrait clip/wipe, bio and CTA stagger. Type-only fallback without photo.
- Work: varied original visual spreads and real approved screenshots; individual image/title/chapter transitions, not stock gallery clone.
- Contact: massive cream wording and burgundy ruled details, responsive links.

Routing graph constraints and synthetic closure; do not present synthetic traffic as real-time data. Use editorial diagrams and genuine interactivity, **not terminal windows**. Keyboard/touch controls, static fallback, reduced-motion behavior.
