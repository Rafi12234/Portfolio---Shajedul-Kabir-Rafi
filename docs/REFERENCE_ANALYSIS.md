# Supplied Editorial Reference — Detailed Analysis

**Source:** Owner-provided vertical creative portfolio screenshot (736 × 1307), October 20, 2026. This document records an art-direction interpretation, not ownership of the image.

## Dominant visual anatomy

1. **Thin utility header:** very small brand/status text and near-invisible horizontal rule, giving scale to the upcoming masthead.
2. **Oversized poster headline:** condensed heavy warm-cream capital letters almost span the full image width. Dominant component, not normal heading.
3. **Portrait overlap:** cropped upper-body real human photographic subject visually in front of masthead; huge burgundy circular backing gives depth and focus.
4. **Supporting identity zones:** discipline at lower-left, bold person's name and concise bio at right, carefully aligned with portrait.
5. **Micro-editorial rules and marks:** warm red lines, small stamps, signatures and technical-looking folio indexes; restrained, not eye-catching UI kits.
6. **Panel rhythm:** "What I Do" row; "My Process" beside "Featured Work"; "About" beside "Tools"; final contact grid. Thin burgundy borders align from cell to cell.
7. **Color discipline:** near-black background, dark wine and burgundy, warm ivory, brown-taupe secondary copy, sparse muted red. No green/blue/purple.
8. **Hierarchical scale:** one enormous word, several mid-sized condensed subheads, highly controlled sans-serif paragraphs, tiny metadata.
9. **Photographic richness:** warm low-key lighting, dark clothing and dark-red forms.
10. **Dense-but-curated page:** large cover provides breathing room, content below is precise editorial collage.

## Typography roles

High-impact extra-condensed bold upper display (candidate Big Shoulders Display 800–900); elegant short serif accent (Instrument Serif); readable UI/text (Instrument Sans); tracked micro-labels. Minuscule text in reference is **NOT** an acceptable mobile body-text size. Add optical scaling and appropriate contrast.

## What to interpret for Rafi

- New original giant masthead/name/phrase tied to Rafi, not a copy of "PORTFOLIO" composition.
- **Real portrait of Rafi only**, with owner's approval; never generate a believable false portrait.
- Same rich black/burgundy/cream and controlled collage rhythm, but uniquely arranged project and research sections.
- True products/systems replace sample designer thumbnails; authentic contributions and statuses replace sample statistics.
- More breathable real content with accessibility tested mobile typography.
- Keep the strongest effects for hero, selected work and finale; maintain generous scroll/interaction smoothness.

## What NOT to reproduce

Original person's name, photo, QR codes, project imagery, actual copy, identical grid arrangement, trademark-like stamps, precise positions or unreadable tiny print. Avoid near-pixel clones.

## Responsive translation

Desktop: large overlap possible, thin grid rules and asymmetric columns. Tablet: adjust headline crop, portrait scale and biography positions. Mobile: compose anew with legible type, portrait and CTA; no hide-and-shrink desktop screenshot. No horizontal overflow at 320px.

## Quality test

At 3 seconds: striking luxury-editorial identity. At 10 seconds: clear that Rafi is a software engineer. At 60 seconds: selected work and professional proof are discoverable. With motion disabled: layout still compelling. With portrait missing: type-led fallback is intentional, not placeholder stock photography.

## Inspiration versus imitation

Match **mood, palette, typographic ambition and motion sophistication**, not specific identity, copyrighted assets, layout geometry or raw design composition. A distinct work portfolio is a requirement.
