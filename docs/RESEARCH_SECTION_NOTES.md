# Section 05 · Research & Publications

This chapter is designed to sit **between Selected Projects and Experience**.

## Content philosophy

The section intentionally distinguishes among:
- **Accepted & presented** work
- **Submitted / under review** work
- **Proposal submitted** work
- **Ongoing investigation**

No submission is styled like a published paper, and no ongoing investigation is framed as a completed publication.

## Interaction model

- A cinematic editorial detail panel sits on the **left**.
- A dynamic research-card field sits on the **right**.
- Clicking any card updates the research dossier with smooth GSAP transitions.
- Status filter buttons allow visitors to isolate accepted work, submissions, proposals, or ongoing research.
- Entrance motion happens **once** on section reveal. No continuous scroll-scrubbing is used, which helps overall smoothness.

## Styling notes

- Same burgundy / crimson / champagne portfolio palette.
- Big watermark words in the background, but content remains readable.
- The left panel is information-dense but still editorial.
- All category badges use clear, distinct colors while staying within the site theme.

## Accuracy reminders

Before public deployment, replace placeholder text where needed:
- official paper title(s)

The current implementation is accurate to the evidence provided in chat and keeps ambiguous metadata clearly marked.
