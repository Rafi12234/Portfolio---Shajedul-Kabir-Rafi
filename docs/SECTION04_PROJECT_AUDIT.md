## Original visual direction

Reference supplied by owner: a tilted free-floating vertical stack of dark, white and red website cards; kinetic movement without forcing page scroll; on hover it freezes; on click an individual card rotates to disclose case-study notes.

Related references inspected: [Pinterest animated editorial cards](https://uk.pinterest.com/pin/831054937521948783/), [Pinterest 3D stacked cards](https://in.pinterest.com/pin/638737159644329730/), [Awwwards work scroll gallery](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/work-scroll-animation-okcc-labs), [Studio K95 floating portfolio](https://dokle.design/awards/k95-portfolio-floats-klein-blue). The implementation translates the technique to the project’s burgundy, obsidian and parchment identity, not the reference’s gray/bright-orange palette.
